# EditIQ Color Correction & Grading V1

## PURPOSE

Apply consistent, measurable color correction and grading.

The goal is not to apply a random cinematic look.

The goal is:

1. Correct technical problems.
2. Preserve natural skin tones.
3. Maintain consistent exposure and white balance.
4. Apply a defined visual style only when requested.
5. Produce repeatable results.

---

# PROCESSING ORDER

Always process color in this order:

1. Exposure
2. White balance
3. Contrast
4. Highlights
5. Shadows
6. Saturation
7. Skin tone correction
8. Color consistency
9. Creative grade

Never apply the creative grade before basic correction.

---

# EXPOSURE

Analyze:

- overall brightness
- clipped highlights
- crushed shadows
- subject exposure

Adjust only when required.

Avoid excessive brightness or artificial HDR appearance.
